# Advisor Meeting Prep

Help prepare for advisor/supervisor meetings by generating structured agendas, summarizing progress, and organizing discussion points.

## Instructions

### Before a meeting:
1. Use `get_recent_papers(days=7)` to see what was read since last meeting
2. Check Obsidian notes for recent research progress
3. Look for any saved ideas in `Research/Ideas/`
4. Generate a meeting prep document

### After a meeting:
1. Help record action items and feedback
2. Save to `Daily/Meeting-YYYY-MM-DD.md`
3. Update related idea notes if the advisor gave direction

## Meeting Prep Template

```markdown
# 导师会议 [YYYY-MM-DD]

## 上次会议后完成的事项
- [ ] [Action item 1 — done/in progress]
- [ ] [Action item 2]

## 本周进展
### 阅读
- [Papers read and key takeaways]

### 研究
- [Experiments run, results obtained]
- [Ideas explored]

### 写作
- [Draft progress, if any]

## 想讨论的问题
1. [Question 1 — provide context]
2. [Question 2]
3. [Decision needed on...]

## 需要导师帮助的
- [Specific ask — feedback on draft / direction advice / resource request]

## 下一步计划
- [What you plan to do before next meeting]
```

## Meeting Notes Template (post-meeting)

```markdown
# 会议记录 [YYYY-MM-DD]

## 导师反馈
- [Key feedback points]

## 决定的事项
- [Decisions made]

## Action Items
- [ ] [Task 1 — deadline]
- [ ] [Task 2 — deadline]

## 新方向/建议
- [Any new directions suggested]
```

## Tips
- Keep the prep concise — advisors appreciate students who respect their time
- Lead with the most important/blocked item
- Frame questions as "I'm choosing between A and B, I lean toward A because..." rather than "what should I do"
- Record action items immediately after the meeting
